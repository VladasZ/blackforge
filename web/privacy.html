Privacy policy

Last updated on 21 September 2026. Back to blackforge

blackforge is a mod manager for Valheim. It works without an account. This page says what is stored when you do sign in, and what the app sends in any case.

Without an account

  • The app asks Thunderstore for the list of mods and downloads mods from there.
  • The app asks our site if a newer version of blackforge exists.
  • When the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Sentry. The report holds the error, the app version, details about your system and your IP address.
  • When you send a bug report yourself, it holds the email you type in, your description, a screenshot of the app window and recent log lines. Key presses are included only when you turn that switch on. Typed text is never recorded.
  • This site counts clicks on the download buttons.

When you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It turns on friends and saves your main setup for your other machines. We ask Google only for your basic profile. We never see your Google password, your contacts or your mail.

We store:

  • your Google account id, your email, your name and the link to your profile picture,
  • the username you pick,
  • your friends and your open friend requests,
  • the list of mods in your profile, with their versions,
  • a private copy of your main setup: exact mod and dependency versions, enabled states and portable mod settings, including their default values. Settings that look like secrets or machine-specific paths are excluded. Other machines only install changes when you apply them,
  • the mod settings that you changed from their defaults. A setting that looks like a secret, such as a password, a token or a web address, never leaves your computer,
  • the time the app last reported that your game is running.

On your computer the app keeps one login token in an encrypted file. On the server the token is stored only as a hash. A login ends by itself after 90 days without use.

Who can see it

Anybody who is signed in to blackforge can find you in the friend search by the start of your username. They see your username and your Google profile picture, nothing else. Somebody who is not signed in sees nothing.

The friends you accepted also see your mods, your shared mod settings and if you are in game. Nobody sees your email or your Google name.

Your private saved setup is available only through your own account. Friends cannot read it.

We do not sell your data, we show no ads and we do not use your data for anything but these features.

Where it is kept

Account data is kept on a server we run ourselves. The site is served through Cloudflare. Crash and bug reports are kept at Sentry. Signing in goes through Google.

How to delete it

Signing out in the app ends your login. To delete your account and everything stored with it, write to vladaszakrevskis@gmail.com from the email of your account. We delete it within 30 days. You can also remove the access of blackforge in your Google account at any time.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new text is put on this page with a new date.
